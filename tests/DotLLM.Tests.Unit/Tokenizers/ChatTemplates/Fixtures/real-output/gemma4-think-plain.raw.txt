Let $B$ be the cost of the bat and $L$ be the cost of the ball.

We are given two pieces of information:
1. The total cost is $1.10: B + L = 1.10$
2. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball: $B = L + 1.00$

We need to find the cost of the ball, $L$.

Substitute the second equation into the first equation:
$(L + 1.00) + L = 1.10$
$2L + 1.00 = 1.10$
$2L = 1.10 - 1.00$
$2L = 0.10$
$L = 0.10 / 2$
$L = 0.05$

The cost of the ball is $0.05.

Check the answer:
If $L = 0.05$, then $B = 0.05 + 1.00 = 1.05$.
Total cost: $B + L = 1.05 + 0.05 = 1.10$.
This matches the given information.
